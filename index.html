Secure Banking Workflow

NOVEXA employs audited manual reviews for all deposits and cashouts to maintain solvency and oversight.

📥

Deposit Pipeline

  • Review official verified deposit account details on the authenticated portal.
  • Execute manual bank transfer through your financial institution.
  • Submit transaction reference and depositor information.
  • Admin reviews proof and balance is atomically credited to your wallet.
📤

Withdrawal Pipeline

  • Submit cashout request indicating withdrawal amount and bank destination.
  • Wallet balance is reserved and queued in pending audit state.
  • Compliance officer audits request and performs manual bank payout.
  • Request marked completed and notification dispatched to your dashboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, technical answers regarding NOVEXA platform operations.

NOVEXA is a manual investment platform designed for capital allocation into configured plans with daily returns, 3-tier partner referral commissions, and administrative review of all financial operations.
When you activate an investment plan from your available balance, the plan generates its configured daily percentage. Income is calculated daily based on active contract terms and credited directly to your wallet balance.
Daily yields are processed automatically once per day at 00:00 (midnight). An idempotent database cron mechanism prevents any double-crediting.
After signing in, navigate to the Deposit page to see the verified NOVEXA bank account details. Complete your bank transfer and submit the transaction reference. The administrator verifies the payment and approves the balance credit.
Submit a withdrawal request on the Wallet page indicating your Nigerian bank details. The request is placed in pending review. Once the administrative team completes the payout, the status is updated to completed.
You receive 15% commission on investments made by direct referrals (Level 1), 3% on partners invited by your referrals (Level 2), and 1% on partners invited at Level 3.
Every newly registered user receives a ₦1,000 credit in their wallet immediately upon successful registration. This is awarded once per account via an atomic server-side trigger.
All balance changes—including deposits, daily income, referral commissions, and withdrawals—are tracked with unique reference codes on your personal Transactions page.
You can submit an inquiry through the Support Tickets page on your dashboard or access the official contact options configured in site settings.
